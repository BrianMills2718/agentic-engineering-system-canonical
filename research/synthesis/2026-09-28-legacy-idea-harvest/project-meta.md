## Failure lessons

1. **Recording worked; closure did not.** Measured 2026-09-13/14: the learnings register held 2,161 entries with 4 resolutions (0.2%). `policy_friction.md` held 678 entries with one resolution field. `policy_observability.db` held 73,985 violation rows with **0** dispositions, and violations per run grew from 1,078 to 1,569 in five days. At this revision it is 2,506 entries and still 4 resolutions (`ls learnings/entries | wc -l`, `ls learnings/resolutions`). Evidence: `docs/ops/ADR-2026-09-14-one-feedback-concern-lifecycle.md` §Evidence. Lesson for AES: never add a capture store without an owned open-to-verified-closed path.
2. **Red forever is not a signal.** 36 of 36 recorded daily audits failed, project-meta CI failed 9 consecutive weeks, and the feedback watchdog reported `degraded` without ever alerting. Evidence: same ADR §Evidence; `docs/plans/285_fix_first_control_surface.md` §Gap. The repair was Idea 6.
3. **The measurement store could not be read at the scale it was written.** Hook receipts were written as one file per invocation: 12 GB in about 2.1 million ~675-byte files. Four control triggers report `unknown` because no scan finishes in minutes, and the fragmentation contributed to repeated WSL crashes. Evidence: #1963, #2179. Lesson: design receipt storage for its reader (roll-up, retention, bounded scan) at write time; IS-AES B.6's "written but never read" also has a "cannot be read" form.
4. **Controls that never decide, and the owner switching them all off.** The read-first gate produced 3,926 receipts and 1 block. The AES maintenance hook produced 4,333 no-op receipts. Always-loaded instructions reached about 50 KB before any subtree file, and shell reads re-injected 10 to 54 KB each. "No control has a retirement condition… Nothing has ever forced a retirement." On 2026-09-18 Brian turned off all 54 Claude Code hooks ("they are causing more problems than good", 2026-09-22). Evidence: `docs/plans/285_fix_first_control_surface.md` §Gap; `learnings/entries/lrn-20260926T223036867555Z-805723f243.json`; #2180. This is the strongest verdict in the source on control-heavy enforcement: AES v0.2's minimal-hook stance should need evidence before it grows.
5. **Detection worked; nobody acted on it.** The D5 wrong-when condition ("concerns unclaimed >24h means the original rule was right") fired on 2026-09-15 with 5 of 16 concerns unclaimed and was re-observed up to 68 of 78 unclaimed at 313 hours (#1979, still open). No superseding ADR exists (no `docs/ops/ADR-*` after 2026-09-15). The autonomous concern-repair lane (ADR-09-14 slice 3) has produced one `agent:repair` issue in total. Lesson: a wrong-when checker needs a named responder and a deadline, or it becomes one more write-only queue.
6. **"Backfill gradually" meant never.** `budget` and `retire_when` were documented as required for Measured and Enforced rows. At landing 125 of 125 were missing (commit `97a03e53d6f`); at this revision 0 of 260 rows have either. D3's importance ratings for lessons were never built (no implementing commit since 2026-09-15). All 12 conditions in the ADR's YAML block still read `status: not_automated`, even though `d5-concerns-unclaimed` is evaluated live (#1979). The decision's own contract has gone stale. Lesson: a new required field ships with a first population pass or does not ship.
7. **Advisory policy changed no behaviour, and enforcement was not shown to help either.** "76 advisory policies changed no behaviour" (`docs/ops/ACCRETION_DETECTOR_ROLLOUT_BRIEF.md` §1). The registry is still 135 of 260 `Suggestion`. The predecessor AES's own `natural_experiment.py` measured an enforced-group violation rate of 3.27% against 0.95% unenforced, 3.4× *worse*. Its verification tools were then moved out of the accepted architecture (`docs/designs/AES_REWRITE_BRIEF_2026-08-24.md` §Why not retrofit). Lesson: an AES control must name the measurement that would show it helps.
8. **Coordination machinery was the largest single source of friction.** Friction entries by policy: `cross-agent-coordination` 18, `policy-session-bound-lane-lifecycle` 11, plus about 30 more across `coordination-claims`, `cross-agent-claims`, `worktree-*`, `coordination-mailbox-*` and similar. Free-text policy names split one problem into many clusters that a person had to merge by alias. Evidence: `policy_friction.md` (`grep '^## 20'` counts); `policy/friction_clusters.yaml`. Lesson for AES execution work: budget friction for any claim or lane mechanism, and key feedback to stable rule IDs rather than free-text names.
9. **Vendored copies of control code drifted and broke consumers.** A claims session-identity bug had to be fixed in 17 repos, 6 of them found only by an exhaustive filesystem sweep (`docs/ops/ACCRETION_DETECTOR_COVERAGE.md` §Coverage summary). Vendored claim tooling is still failing in consumers (#2187, #2153, #2135, #2039, #2028), including `agentic-engineering-system-canonical` itself (#2181). Lesson for AES distribution: pin and report drift (IS-AES #22) rather than copy controls into consumers.
10. **Automatic repair overrode a human decision.** An investigation found the hooks missing, searched ADRs, policy and commits but not the user's prompts, concluded "accident", re-enabled all 54, and added SessionStart auto-repair that would have kept re-enabling them. Evidence: #2180; `learnings/entries/lrn-20260926T223036867555Z-805723f243.json`. The repair was Idea 18.
11. **A generated instruction projection silently lost rules.** Project Meta's generated `AGENTS.md` omitted the continuous-execution and policy-feedback sections of its `CLAUDE.md` (repaired in PR #2115). Codex stopped reading instruction files at 32 KiB without warning. Evidence: `docs/plans/291_agents-md-only-instructions.md` §Gap; `docs/plans/285_fix_first_control_surface.md` §Gap. The repair was Idea 17.
12. **Stores died silently.** `verification_gap_log.md` stopped at 2026-08-23 even though a skill "maintains" it. Codex skill feedback had **0** rows. The ecosystem feedback stream file was never created. A 50-minute lesson-recall outage went unrecorded. Evidence: ADR-2026-09-14 §Evidence; `git log -1 -- verification_gap_log.md`. Lesson: every store needs a liveness check ("no write in N days while its producer ran" is red).
13. **The control ran from an untracked source.** Global git hooks under `~/.config/git/hooks` existed only on one machine. A subagent's Makefile overwrote `pre-commit`, and the restore from a transcript copy could not be proven byte-for-byte. Evidence: #2184 (fixed in machine-coordination#76). Lesson: AES-installed hooks must come from a tracked source with a drift check.
14. **Hand-written paths in registries broke on rename.** Capability registry `evidence` paths broke silently when repos moved from `~/projects` to `~/code` and were renamed. Evidence: `docs/ops/CANONICAL_SOURCES_AND_CONSUMER_REPOS.md` (preamble). Lesson: resolve repo locations through one identity authority; do not store paths.

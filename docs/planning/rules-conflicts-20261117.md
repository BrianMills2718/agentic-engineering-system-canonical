But the only words of his behind that line are about delivery by email rather than by post. The core rule since 2026-11-09 lets something go out without him only if he approved that kind of send. So the question is: may agents send new records requests, and follow-ups on requests already sent, from his Gmail without him approving each text? Or must he see each new request first? A recommendation and the exact answer words belong in the reply that carries this page, not here.

**Question 2. Existing personal copies of company repositories.** The never-mirror rule (`topics/outward-accounts-and-cross-project.md:101-103`, 2026-11-06) stops new copies from being proposed. It says nothing about copies that already exist. The question for Brian is whether those should keep being refreshed, be left as they are with the refresh stopped, or be moved aside.

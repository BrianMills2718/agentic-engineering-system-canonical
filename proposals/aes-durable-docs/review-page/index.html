5. What would prove this wrong

  • iPlans dodge it. Two weeks after M4, of the 10 latest finished plans in the two pilot repositories, 5 or more say 'none'.
  • iUpdates are paperwork. Or their document changes only touch IDs, dates or links, judged by reading the diffs.
  • iThe documents don't answer. A fresh agent fails the canonical question ('what rule, who owns it, which check, why') on a pilot the checker calls clean.
  • iarc42 claim broken. A document claiming arc42 section names breaks one of the plan's four heading rules, or an AES addition is unlabelled.

If any of these happens, the plan's feedback entry records it and the rule is revised or withdrawn.

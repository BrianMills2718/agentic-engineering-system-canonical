The right column is what epistemic-warrant already worked out for it, and whether the agent model uses it. 20 of 31 worked-out items are not used.

Key: filled blue dot = used; half blue = partly used; dashed grey = not used; orange square with ⚠ = rebuilt locally or conflicts. Words are always shown next to the mark.

What backs a claim and how strongly: which evidence and assumptions it rests on.

What attacks a claim, and whether the claim survives the attack.
Warrant
Whether the backing is good enough, judged under a named standard.
Licence
What the agent may do with a claim in a given use: state it, build on it, act on it.
Epistemic action / update
How the agent's state of belief changes, including adding or dropping whole hypotheses.
Strategy / control
How the agent decides what to think about, what to run and when to stop.

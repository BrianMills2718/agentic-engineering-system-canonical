1. What it is for

Outcome OUT-WG5-001 for a person asking a bounded "why" question

The person sees the graph challenge the model's causal explanation with a conflict that no prompt asked the model to produce, and sees exactly how the explanation changed in response.

Not trying to do: a world model or truth discovery; general contradiction detection beyond the typed evaluator; a live collaborative UI; feature parity with whygame4 or whygame-reboot

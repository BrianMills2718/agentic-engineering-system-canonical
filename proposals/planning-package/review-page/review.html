1.Goal: what this plan is for and why

Outcome. AES is the whole system (Lockstep, the feedback loop and the policy layer), and Lockstep becomes one configurable, portable, shareable planning-and-checking tool in AES canonical that writes each planned file's first docstring from the approved plan, while the feedback loop and policy layer keep working.

North star

Brian approves a plan once, and from then on agents in any of his repositories, personal or Inside Success, lay out, write and commit code that stays in line with it, while his corrections and rules keep improving how they work.

Thesis

One tool reading one plan format does the job of three overlapping tools with less to install, configure and keep in step, and loses nothing any of them did.

What would prove it wrong

Wrong if, after slice 6, a repository needs code2check, pcw or Company Planning's own plan check for something lockstep does not do, or if any acceptance entry that passed before a slice fails after it.

Not for

Not a return of universal commit enforcement (removed 2026-10-14). Not the retirement of `.aes/` in other repositories (decision 0009 (a), one at a time). Not new feedback-loop jobs (Q21).

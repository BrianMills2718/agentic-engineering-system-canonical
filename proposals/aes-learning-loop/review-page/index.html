Agent feedback loop: reports become enforced rules, and we check they worked

Every lesson, friction, problem, experiment and research finding an agent meets becomes a short report that links to its evidence. The loop sorts and groups reports, works out causes later, turns well-supported claims into rules or checks you approve, then counts whether the failure stops.

Three kinds of record (replacing lesson / friction / problem)

ObservationSomething seen, with links. A "problem" is one that contradicts an expectation; "friction" is one about a rule or tool.
ClaimWhat the agent now believes: seen / inferred / guessed, a confidence, and links to what supports or challenges it. A "lesson" is a claim with an active licence.
ActionInvestigate (experiments, research), Mitigate, Repair, Detect or Prevent, with its result linked.

What you see

A page ranked by impact (how many sessions and repositories a problem hits, how often, what it cost) and then uncertainty (how thin the evidence is, whether a rule's effect is measured yet). High impact with high uncertainty is at the top. You are notified when a high-impact rule is adopted or a rule is revoked, plus a weekly one-line digest. Nothing asks you to approve.

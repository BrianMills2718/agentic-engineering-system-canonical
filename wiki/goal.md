# What is AES for?

AES helps autonomous coding agents deliver software that stays true to its intent.
It combines Lockstep's linked planning/code/documentation, a feedback loop and a
policy layer. Alignment is a means to effective autonomous agents.

Read the [current purpose, thesis, example and boundaries](../docs/architecture/GOAL.md).

- [Lockstep](lockstep.md): plan behavior, file structure, interfaces and tests;
  project requirements down and implementation status up.
- [Feedback](feedback-loop.md): disposition observed failures and verify repairs.
- [Policy](policy-and-rules.md): locate rules and their enforcement.
- [Documentation roles](../docs/architecture/DOCUMENTATION.md): find each fact's authority.
- [Current state](../.project-brain/now.md) and [open discussion](open-questions.md).
- [History](history.md): earlier greenfield outcomes and retired governance.

This page is curated navigation to its owning sources. Its existence does not
prove that the complete projection loop is automatic in every consumer.

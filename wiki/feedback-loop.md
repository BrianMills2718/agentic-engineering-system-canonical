# The feedback loop

The feedback loop is part 2 of AES; it is kept and runs nightly from `scripts/learning_loop` in this repository ([.project-brain/now.md:9](https://github.com/BrianMills2718/agentic-engineering-system-canonical/blob/b0d4cbd174e78c075eed5248beb00509d636d0b0/.project-brain/now.md#L9)).
Code2-workspace decision 0009 keeps `collect_feedback.py`, `label_items.py` and `feedback_log.py` running ([docs/decisions/0009-aes-and-lockstep-fate.md:59-61](https://github.com/BrianMills2718/code2-workspace/blob/b8b523d7ec59f54248dac2d7a780441b787d6921/docs/decisions/0009-aes-and-lockstep-fate.md#L59-L61)).

## Why it exists

- Earlier systems recorded lessons and rarely closed the loop: the legacy register held 2,572 learnings with 4 resolutions, and no rule was ever retired ([proposals/aes-learning-loop/DESIGN.md:22-25](https://github.com/BrianMills2718/agentic-engineering-system-canonical/blob/37dc876cc3dd7f88ece46873f17a0015228af123/proposals/aes-learning-loop/DESIGN.md#L22-L25)).
- Goal: each lesson, friction, problem, experiment and research finding becomes a short linked report; the loop sorts reports, analyses recurring problems, turns licensed claims into enforced rules or checks, and observes whether the failure stops recurring ([proposals/aes-learning-loop/PLAN.md:9](https://github.com/BrianMills2718/agentic-engineering-system-canonical/blob/37dc876cc3dd7f88ece46873f17a0015228af123/proposals/aes-learning-loop/PLAN.md#L9)).
- A lesson or rule counts only once a check enforces it; until then it is a note ([proposals/aes-learning-loop/PLAN.md:48](https://github.com/BrianMills2718/agentic-engineering-system-canonical/blob/37dc876cc3dd7f88ece46873f17a0015228af123/proposals/aes-learning-loop/PLAN.md#L48)).
- Agents adopt and retire rules by their best judgment; Brian observes a view ranked by impact and uncertainty and is notified ([proposals/aes-learning-loop/PLAN.md:47](https://github.com/BrianMills2718/agentic-engineering-system-canonical/blob/37dc876cc3dd7f88ece46873f17a0015228af123/proposals/aes-learning-loop/PLAN.md#L47)).
- The 2026-10-02 design had rules proposed to Brian for decision; his 2026-10-07 direction superseded that, so agents adopt and retire rules ([proposals/aes-learning-loop/PLAN.md:49](https://github.com/BrianMills2718/agentic-engineering-system-canonical/blob/37dc876cc3dd7f88ece46873f17a0015228af123/proposals/aes-learning-loop/PLAN.md#L49)).

## Records and reports

- Every record has three parts: one sentence, links to where it came from, and who filed it when ([proposals/aes-learning-loop/PLAN.md:122-123](https://github.com/BrianMills2718/agentic-engineering-system-canonical/blob/37dc876cc3dd7f88ece46873f17a0015228af123/proposals/aes-learning-loop/PLAN.md#L122-L123)).
- The record kinds are observation, claim (with basis, confidence and evidence word) and action (Investigate, Mitigate, Repair, Detect or Prevent) ([proposals/aes-learning-loop/PLAN.md:125-135](https://github.com/BrianMills2718/agentic-engineering-system-canonical/blob/37dc876cc3dd7f88ece46873f17a0015228af123/proposals/aes-learning-loop/PLAN.md#L125-L135)).
- Agents write the closeout Feedback field with `obs`, `claim` and `action` lines ([proposals/aes-learning-loop/PLAN.md:143-151](https://github.com/BrianMills2718/agentic-engineering-system-canonical/blob/37dc876cc3dd7f88ece46873f17a0015228af123/proposals/aes-learning-loop/PLAN.md#L143-L151)).
- A line with no resolvable link is kept but marked `unprovenanced` and can never support a licence ([proposals/aes-learning-loop/PLAN.md:153-155](https://github.com/BrianMills2718/agentic-engineering-system-canonical/blob/37dc876cc3dd7f88ece46873f17a0015228af123/proposals/aes-learning-loop/PLAN.md#L153-L155)).
- A bare `#N` link is qualified with the repository of the session's working folder, so a link meant for another repository is wrong but still counted as a link; agents should write `owner/repo#N` ([proposals/aes-learning-loop/PLAN.md:275-277](https://github.com/BrianMills2718/agentic-engineering-system-canonical/blob/37dc876cc3dd7f88ece46873f17a0015228af123/proposals/aes-learning-loop/PLAN.md#L275-L277)).
- A licence is derived, never set: `active` needs two independent supporting observations and no unanswered challenge ([proposals/aes-learning-loop/PLAN.md:137-141](https://github.com/BrianMills2718/agentic-engineering-system-canonical/blob/37dc876cc3dd7f88ece46873f17a0015228af123/proposals/aes-learning-loop/PLAN.md#L137-L141)).
- The contract is `feedback-report.v1`, a Pydantic model in `scripts/learning_loop/records.py` ([proposals/aes-learning-loop/PLAN.md:230-231](https://github.com/BrianMills2718/agentic-engineering-system-canonical/blob/37dc876cc3dd7f88ece46873f17a0015228af123/proposals/aes-learning-loop/PLAN.md#L230-L231)).
- The log is GitHub issues in a private repository, one issue per problem ([proposals/aes-learning-loop/PLAN.md:189-193](https://github.com/BrianMills2718/agentic-engineering-system-canonical/blob/37dc876cc3dd7f88ece46873f17a0015228af123/proposals/aes-learning-loop/PLAN.md#L189-L193)); the collector files to `BrianMills2718/agent-feedback-log` ([scripts/learning_loop/systemd/README.md:55-56](../scripts/learning_loop/systemd/README.md#L55-L56)).

## Flow

- Report at work time, extract nightly, sort with Jev, group and analyse weekly, licence and decide, enforce, observe the effect nightly ([proposals/aes-learning-loop/PLAN.md:157-176](https://github.com/BrianMills2718/agentic-engineering-system-canonical/blob/37dc876cc3dd7f88ece46873f17a0015228af123/proposals/aes-learning-loop/PLAN.md#L157-L176)).
- A rule whose failure recurs revokes its licence and reopens the problem ([proposals/aes-learning-loop/PLAN.md:174-176](https://github.com/BrianMills2718/agentic-engineering-system-canonical/blob/37dc876cc3dd7f88ece46873f17a0015228af123/proposals/aes-learning-loop/PLAN.md#L174-L176)).

## Collector

- It reads Claude Code and Codex transcripts from the last day and turns each closeout Feedback field (and the older Learnings heading) into one report filed as one issue ([scripts/learning_loop/systemd/README.md:53-56](../scripts/learning_loop/systemd/README.md#L53-L56)).
- The report cap was raised from 80 to 900 a night on 2026-11-10, when 10,652 reports were waiting ([scripts/learning_loop/systemd/README.md:57-58](../scripts/learning_loop/systemd/README.md#L57-L58)).
- Exit 1 means some model or filing calls failed and exit 2 a crash; both fail the unit ([scripts/learning_loop/systemd/README.md:59-60](../scripts/learning_loop/systemd/README.md#L59-L60)).
- Brian's own corrections become reports with the literal quote, deduplicated by quote ([scripts/learning_loop/systemd/README.md:62-66](../scripts/learning_loop/systemd/README.md#L62-L66)).
- Subagent results are joined from parent tool events and kept unverified until a parent check runs ([scripts/learning_loop/systemd/README.md:69-74](../scripts/learning_loop/systemd/README.md#L69-L74)).
- A failed checker yields `verification_failed`, not a judgment of the child ([scripts/learning_loop/systemd/README.md:84-86](../scripts/learning_loop/systemd/README.md#L84-L86)).
- At most 10 Learnings lines a night go to the project-meta register, skipping repeats ([scripts/learning_loop/systemd/README.md:93-97](../scripts/learning_loop/systemd/README.md#L93-L97)).
- Why the filing gate looks this way: closeout Learnings lines were 8/8 reusable against 7/25 for narration, and Jev's yes/no answer did not separate them ([scripts/learning_loop/systemd/README.md:101-104](../scripts/learning_loop/systemd/README.md#L101-L104)) ([datasets/learning-loop/experiments/2026-10-07-filing-gate/README.md:9](https://github.com/BrianMills2718/agentic-engineering-system-canonical/blob/b0d4cbd174e78c075eed5248beb00509d636d0b0/datasets/learning-loop/experiments/2026-10-07-filing-gate/README.md#L9)).
- The first production run, 2026-10-08 03:18, read 83 reports (55 Claude, 28 Codex), filed 40 issues and wrote 69 records, 22 of them without a link (all written before the line grammar shipped), with 0 errors for $0.024 ([proposals/aes-learning-loop/PLAN.md:266](https://github.com/BrianMills2718/agentic-engineering-system-canonical/blob/37dc876cc3dd7f88ece46873f17a0015228af123/proposals/aes-learning-loop/PLAN.md#L266)).
- Measured costs: Jev about $0.0001 per question, and the light extraction model about $0.14 for three days of transcripts (2026-10-06) ([proposals/aes-learning-loop/PLAN.md:66-68](https://github.com/BrianMills2718/agentic-engineering-system-canonical/blob/37dc876cc3dd7f88ece46873f17a0015228af123/proposals/aes-learning-loop/PLAN.md#L66-L68)).
- This repository is public, so no transcript text or extracted quote is ever committed here, and the tests use synthetic transcripts ([proposals/aes-learning-loop/DESIGN.md:197-199](https://github.com/BrianMills2718/agentic-engineering-system-canonical/blob/37dc876cc3dd7f88ece46873f17a0015228af123/proposals/aes-learning-loop/DESIGN.md#L197-L199)).
- The original collector design was to count as wrong if a spot check of 10 filed entries found fewer than 7 worth keeping, or if the nightly cost passed $0.50 ([proposals/aes-learning-loop/DESIGN.md:201-203](https://github.com/BrianMills2718/agentic-engineering-system-canonical/blob/37dc876cc3dd7f88ece46873f17a0015228af123/proposals/aes-learning-loop/DESIGN.md#L201-L203)).

- The labels job gives each open feedback issue a `family:<letter>`, `fact` or `other` label; two or more open `other` items open the `taxonomy:unmatched-failures` concern in agent-skills ([scripts/learning_loop/systemd/README.md:20](../scripts/learning_loop/systemd/README.md#L20)).
- It replaced agent-skills' weekly taxonomy report on 2026-10-26, after which nothing new had reached the failure-mode list since 2026-10-13 ([scripts/learning_loop/systemd/README.md:28-30](../scripts/learning_loop/systemd/README.md#L28-L30)).
- The families come from [docs/failure-modes.md](../docs/failure-modes.md), the current taxonomy authority ([docs/failure-modes.md:18-20](../docs/failure-modes.md#L18-L20)).
- A new family needs two independent instances ([docs/failure-modes.md:61-63](../docs/failure-modes.md#L61-L63)).
- The classifier is Jev (`typesafe/jev-1.13`) with frozen question set v2 ([datasets/learning-loop/README.md:9-10](https://github.com/BrianMills2718/agentic-engineering-system-canonical/blob/b0d4cbd174e78c075eed5248beb00509d636d0b0/datasets/learning-loop/README.md#L9-L10)) ([datasets/learning-loop/README.md:19](https://github.com/BrianMills2718/agentic-engineering-system-canonical/blob/b0d4cbd174e78c075eed5248beb00509d636d0b0/datasets/learning-loop/README.md#L19)).

## Weekly analysis, effects and fix owner (when switched on)

- Weekly analysis groups 14 days of reports into problems, analyses each recurring one with a stronger model, and derives licences; each active licence opens one "Feedback loop: adopt" concern ([scripts/learning_loop/systemd/README.md:110-127](../scripts/learning_loop/systemd/README.md#L110-L127)).
- The analysis runs on the Claude subscription as `claude-code/sonnet` ([scripts/learning_loop/systemd/feedback-problems.service:20-22](../scripts/learning_loop/systemd/feedback-problems.service#L20-L22)).
- On OpenRouter the analysis cost about $0.017 per problem (2026-10-11); on the subscription it takes about 90 s each, and every recurring problem is analysed with no weekly cap (Brian, 2026-10-12) ([proposals/aes-learning-loop/PLAN.md:67-70](https://github.com/BrianMills2718/agentic-engineering-system-canonical/blob/37dc876cc3dd7f88ece46873f17a0015228af123/proposals/aes-learning-loop/PLAN.md#L67-L70)).
- The S2 measurement (2026-10-08) of relation judging on 30 hand-labelled pairs scored Jev 24 of 30 acceptable for $0.0006 and `openai/gpt-5.6-sol` 27 of 30 for $0.03 ([proposals/aes-learning-loop/PLAN.md:326-329](https://github.com/BrianMills2718/agentic-engineering-system-canonical/blob/37dc876cc3dd7f88ece46873f17a0015228af123/proposals/aes-learning-loop/PLAN.md#L326-L329)).
- So Jev judges relations nightly, but a Jev `challenges` answer is confirmed by the stronger model before it counts, because Jev's only two `challenges` answers were both wrong ([proposals/aes-learning-loop/PLAN.md:331-333](https://github.com/BrianMills2718/agentic-engineering-system-canonical/blob/37dc876cc3dd7f88ece46873f17a0015228af123/proposals/aes-learning-loop/PLAN.md#L331-L333)).

- A verified recurrence after enforcement revokes the licence and reopens the concern once ([scripts/learning_loop/systemd/README.md:133-136](../scripts/learning_loop/systemd/README.md#L133-L136)).

## What remains intentionally separate

- **Technical/evidence conformance** — tests, exact revision, negative/error behavior, provenance, authentic external execution.
- **Stakeholder utility** — A1 direct use of the generated surface and `continue | change | stop`.
- **Gap closure** — only fresh post-implementation characterization and recomputation can close or narrow originating gaps.

A green automated check cannot prove the surface is useful. A useful surface cannot excuse missing or failed technical evidence.

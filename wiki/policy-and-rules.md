# Policy and rules

## Where the rules live

- The rules register is in this repository (`scripts/rules`); the rest of the policy (core.md and topic files) lives in code2-workspace `agents/` ([.project-brain/now.md:9](../.project-brain/now.md#L9)).
- [docs/rules/RULES.md](../docs/rules/RULES.md) is generated by `scripts/rules/build_rules_page.py` from the register `docs/rules/register.yaml`; edit the inputs, not the page ([docs/rules/RULES.md:3](../docs/rules/RULES.md#L3)).
- The statuses are enforced, partially-enforced, checked, advisory, licensed and withdrawn ([docs/rules/RULES.md:9](../docs/rules/RULES.md#L9)).
- The legacy project-meta register inventory proposes 229 keep, 27 covered and 7 retire, and no mandatory legacy rule is removed or activated by it ([docs/rules/RULES.md:21-23](../docs/rules/RULES.md#L21-L23)).
- A rule check (`policies.py`, run after the effects job) checks every Policy id the workspace AGENTS.md names for a WORKING_RULES source entry and a register entry, and opens the keyed concern `workspace-policy-unregistered` for a gap ([scripts/learning_loop/systemd/README.md:149-152](../scripts/learning_loop/systemd/README.md#L149-L152)).
- That check last reported 9 rules unregistered ([proposals/aes-learning-loop/PLAN.md:436](../proposals/aes-learning-loop/PLAN.md#L436)); status as last written, not verified.

## Standing policies recorded here

- Apparent novelty is unresolved search debt, not a positive design signal; custom construction is a last-resort bounded residual ([.project-brain/now.md:153](../.project-brain/now.md#L153)).
- That policy is encoded in SYSTEM_BOUNDARY AES-CAP-002 and in the plan template ([.project-brain/now.md:155](../.project-brain/now.md#L155)).
- Off-the-shelf first: adopt or configure existing tools before building ([docs/architecture/GOAL.md:25-26](../docs/architecture/GOAL.md#L25-L26)).
- Truth lives where it is edited: a fact about one artifact is authored in that artifact and wiki pages are generated from it ([docs/architecture/SYSTEM_BOUNDARY.md:131](../docs/architecture/SYSTEM_BOUNDARY.md#L131)); see [Decision 0013](../docs/decisions/0013-truth-lives-where-it-is-edited.md).
- Capability reuse is checked against one catalogue ([docs/decisions/0012-capability-reuse-enforced-at-plan-adoption.md:42-45](../docs/decisions/0012-capability-reuse-enforced-at-plan-adoption.md#L42-L45)); see [Decision 0012](../docs/decisions/0012-capability-reuse-enforced-at-plan-adoption.md).
- A block must provide a runnable recovery path or an explicit human escalation boundary ([CLAUDE.md:117](../CLAUDE.md#L117)).
- Changes to the adopted methodology go through `proposals/` and then the owning methodology repository ([CLAUDE.md:121](../CLAUDE.md#L121)).

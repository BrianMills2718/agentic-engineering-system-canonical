## The rules register (current)

- [docs/rules/RULES.md](../docs/rules/RULES.md) is generated by `scripts/rules/build_rules_page.py` from [docs/rules/register.yaml](../docs/rules/register.yaml); edit the inputs, not the page. [docs/rules/RULES.md:3](../docs/rules/RULES.md#L3)
- `build_rules_page.py --check` exits 1 when the page is out of date. [scripts/rules/build_rules_page.py:9-10](../scripts/rules/build_rules_page.py#L9-L10)
- Full legacy source text is rendered only with `--private` and an output outside the public repository. [scripts/rules/build_rules_page.py:4-7](../scripts/rules/build_rules_page.py#L4-L7)
- Statuses: enforced, partially-enforced, checked, advisory, licensed (from the feedback loop, not yet adopted) and withdrawn. [docs/rules/RULES.md:9](../docs/rules/RULES.md#L9)
- The published page holds one named workspace rule, `recommendations-not-question-lists`, advisory: settle what checking can settle, then give Brian approvable recommendations. [docs/rules/RULES.md:17](../docs/rules/RULES.md#L17)
- The legacy project-meta register's inventory proposes 229 keep, 27 covered and 7 retire; no mandatory legacy rule is removed or activated by it. [docs/rules/RULES.md:21-23](../docs/rules/RULES.md#L21-L23)
- `scripts/rules/sort_legacy.py` sorts legacy rules into covered, keep or retire; its default is an offline check, and model sorting needs separate authority. [scripts/rules/sort_legacy.py:1-23](../scripts/rules/sort_legacy.py#L1-L23)
- The feedback loop's `policies.py` reads this register as current; project-meta `policy/registry.yaml` is legacy. [scripts/learning_loop/policies.py:7-10](../scripts/learning_loop/policies.py#L7-L10)
- The failure-mode taxonomy used for labels is [docs/failure-modes.md](../docs/failure-modes.md), a list of families each with a detecting question. [docs/failure-modes.md:4-5](../docs/failure-modes.md#L4-L5)

## The agent router (proposal, runtime acceptance pending)

- Brian, 2026-10-08: a fast model could choose each job's subagent, extra injected rules, model and effort. [proposals/agent-router/README.md:15-19](../proposals/agent-router/README.md#L15-L19)
- Rules come in four layers: a fixed safety core always loaded, role rules, action rules injected by a hook before a tool call, and intent rules chosen by the router. [proposals/agent-router/README.md:58-63](../proposals/agent-router/README.md#L58-L63)
- Model and effort selection reuses llm_client Plan #379 rather than a second selector, and the safety core is never left to the router. [proposals/agent-router/README.md:95-96](../proposals/agent-router/README.md#L95-L96)
- Slices: tag and sort legacy rules, an action hook, an observe-only router, then a bounded canary before any routing proposal. [proposals/agent-router/README.md:82-85](../proposals/agent-router/README.md#L82-L85)
- As last written (2026-10-09) the plan was adopted, all eight acceptance criteria were planned and runtime acceptance was pending. [proposals/agent-router/README.md:3](../proposals/agent-router/README.md#L3)
- `scripts/rules/agent_router.py` holds the profile and handoff accounting contracts; native delivery and outcome checks stay separate. [scripts/rules/agent_router.py:1-5](../scripts/rules/agent_router.py#L1-L5)
- Required rules must be mapped to a delivery route, a deterministic gate and a fallback in all four client/agent cells before always-loaded instructions shrink. [proposals/agent-router/README.md:100](../proposals/agent-router/README.md#L100)
